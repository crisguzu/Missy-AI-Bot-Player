# Twitch demos

**No verified Twitch clip or VOD links have been selected for this showcase yet.** No synthetic screenshot is presented here as gameplay.

## Planned demo chapters

1. **Meet Missy_AI:** normal interaction and the boundary between conversation and action.
2. **A short outing and Home return:** show departure, physical travel and the complete return in context.
3. **Human Stop:** show cancellation and the resulting idle state without promising instant network delivery.
4. **A failure and what we learned:** show a held or blocked outcome honestly; explain why uncertain work is not blindly retried.

These are editorial targets, not claims that corresponding recordings already exist.

## Each published entry will include

- A verified public Twitch clip or VOD URL and timestamp range.
- Recording date and the behavior visible in that recording.
- Whether it is genuine gameplay, a synthetic test or a reenactment.
- What succeeded, what failed, relevant edits and what the clip does not prove.

Use only footage cleared for sharing. Keep credentials, private dashboards, server addresses and private chat out of selected clips. If those cannot be safely omitted, choose a different segment. Link to selected Twitch material rather than redistributing music or third-party video assets.
